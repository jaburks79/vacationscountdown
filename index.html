How to use the vacation countdown

Enter your destination and vacation start date, then press Start countdown. The timer counts down in real time — days, hours, minutes, and seconds — until you leave. Bookmark the page and check back daily to watch the excitement build.

Why Count Down to Your Vacation?

There's something almost magical about watching the days tick down to a trip you've been dreaming about. Whether it's a beach in Cancun, a cruise through the Caribbean, or a first trip to Europe, a vacation countdown makes the anticipation part of the experience. Studies show that anticipating a positive event can boost mood just as much as the event itself — so start counting down and enjoy every day between now and departure.

VacationsCountdown is completely free, works on any device, and requires no account or app download. Just enter your date and go.

Will it remember my vacation?

Check the "Remember my vacation" box and your destination and date will be saved on your device. Next time you visit, your countdown picks up right where it left off — no re-entering needed.

What trips is this good for?

Any trip you're excited about — beach vacations, cruises, Disney trips, international travel, ski trips, road trips, honeymoons, family reunions, and more. If you have a departure date, this tool is for you.

Can I share my countdown?

Just share the link to vacationscountdown.com with your travel companions and have them enter the same date. Everyone can count down together from their own device.

How far in advance can I set it?

As far out as you'd like. Planning a trip a year from now? No problem. The countdown will tick down from years all the way to the final seconds before you leave.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — VacationsCountdown is fully mobile-responsive and works on any smartphone or tablet. Bookmark it to your home screen so you can check the countdown anytime, anywhere.

Is it free?

Completely free, always. No account, no subscription, no app download required. Just open it, enter your vacation date, and start counting down.
