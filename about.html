About VacationsCountdown

VacationsCountdown.com is a free vacation countdown timer built for anyone with a trip on the calendar and too many days left to wait. Enter your destination and departure date, and the timer counts down in real time — days, hours, minutes, and seconds — until you leave.

Why we built it

The weeks and days before a big trip can feel endless. We built VacationsCountdown to make that waiting feel exciting instead of frustrating. Research consistently shows that anticipating a positive event can boost your mood just as much as the event itself — so why not lean into it? Having a live countdown on your phone or browser turns every day into a small milestone on the way to somewhere great.

What it does

The tool is intentionally simple. Enter your destination (optional) and your vacation start date, hit the button, and watch the countdown run. Check the "Remember my vacation" box and your details are saved to your device — so the next time you visit, your countdown is right where you left it. No account, no app, no signup required.

Who it's for

Anyone counting down to a trip — beach vacations, cruises, theme park visits, international travel, ski trips, honeymoons, road trips, or family reunions. If you've got a date on the calendar and you're excited about it, this is for you.

Free, always

VacationsCountdown is and always will be completely free. The site is supported by advertising so we can keep the lights on without charging users. We hope the countdown adds a little joy to your pre-trip days — enjoy the anticipation, and enjoy every second of your trip.
